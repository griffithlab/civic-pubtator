Tumor necrosis factor alpha (TNF-α) and interleukin-6 (IL-6) were significantly elevated in patients treated with methotrexate (MTX) compared to those receiving cyclophosphamide (CYC). Epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) expression was assessed before and after treatment with 5-fluorouracil (5-FU). Inhibition of vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) by bevacizumab reduced downstream activation of mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK). Patients with mutant Kirsten rat sarcoma viral proto-oncogene (KRAS) showed reduced response to cetuximab (CTX), whereas those with wild-type KRAS responded well. Deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) damage induced by cisplatin (CDDP) was measured via the comet assay.
